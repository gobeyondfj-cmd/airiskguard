# Services & Contact

## How We Can Help

airiskguard provides expert services to help organizations build, assess, and maintain AI risk governance programs aligned with international standards.

### Consulting

Work with our AI governance specialists to assess your current risk posture, identify gaps, and build a roadmap to compliance.

- **AI Risk Assessment** — Evaluate your AI systems against AIRMS v1.0 and receive a detailed gap analysis with prioritized remediation steps
- **Regulatory Readiness** — Prepare for EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and ISO/IEC 42001 compliance with expert guidance
- **Risk Framework Design** — Design and implement an AI risk management framework tailored to your organization's size, industry, and risk appetite
- **Policy Development** — Draft AI governance policies, procedures, and RACI matrices for your organization

### Implementation

Get hands-on help deploying airiskguard in your production environment.

- **Integration Engineering** — Integrate airiskguard into your LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and agent systems
- **Custom Checker Development** — Build domain-specific risk checkers for your industry (healthcare, finance, legal, etc.)
- **CI/CD Pipeline Integration** — Embed automated risk assessments into your development and deployment workflows
- **Dashboard & Reporting Setup** — Configure audit trails, compliance dashboards, and regulatory report generation

### Training & Enablement

Empower your team with the knowledge and skills to manage AI risk independently.

- **AI Risk Management Training** — Workshops for engineering, compliance, and leadership teams
- **Standard Certification Prep** — Prepare your team for ISO/IEC 42001 and other AI governance certifications
- **Red Team Exercises** — Guided adversarial testing sessions to evaluate your AI system's robustness

### Enterprise & Partnership

- **Enterprise Licensing** — Custom licensing and support agreements for large-scale deployments
- **Technology Partnerships** — Integrate airiskguard into your platform or product offering
- **Research Collaboration** — Joint research on emerging AI risks, new detection methods, and standard development
